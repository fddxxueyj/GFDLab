Introduction

Welcome to Python-GFDLab!

My name is Yanjun Xue, and I am an undergraduate student in Atmospheric Science at Fudan University. My research interests lie in atmospheric dynamics and physical oceanography, as well as the numerical modeling of these phenomena.

This website serves as a space to share my personal projects in simulating Geophysical Fluid Dynamics. These materials are freely available for educational purposes (actually encouraged !), as I have always believed that the current GFD courses are filled with either tedious mathematical derivations or vague qualitative description, while lacking intuitive physical illustrations and quantitative analysis :(
