Introduction

Welcome to GFDLab!

My name is Yanjun Xue, and I am an undergraduate student in Atmospheric Science at Fudan University. My research interests lie in geophysical fluid dynamics, physical oceanography, and computational modeling of the atmosphere and ocean.

This website serves as a space to share my personal projects in geophysical fluid dynamics and their numerical simulations. These materials are freely available for educational purposes (and their use is actually encouraged!), as I have always believed that the current geophysical fluid dynamics courses are overly filled with mathematical derivations, while lacking intuitive physical illustrations.
