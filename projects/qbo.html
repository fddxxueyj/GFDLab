Introduction

This page introduces the Quasi-Biennial Oscillation (QBO) and its numerical modeling. The Quasi-Biennial Oscillation is the dominant climate variability in the tropical lower stratosphere characterized by descending eastward and westward jets that alternate with a period of about 28 months.

The first successful theory was proposed by Lindzen and Holton in 1968. They attributed the downward propagation of the QBO to the absorption of gravity waves at critical levels. To briefly explain this theory, we should set aside the QBO and introduce the EP flux (Eliassen and Palm, 1960). Considering the motion in z-x plane and there exists a mean flow \( U \). The energy flux due to the wave holds the following equation:

\[ \overbar{p'w'} = -\rho_0 (U-c) \overbar{u'w'} \]
